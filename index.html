How to Manage Everyday Waste

Plastic

Empty and clean recyclable plastic containers. Give them to an appropriate waste collector.

Paper

Collect newspapers, notebooks and cardboard separately for recycling.

Food Waste

Use fruit peels, vegetable scraps and suitable garden waste for composting.

Electronic Waste

Give old phones, chargers and electronic devices to authorised e-waste collection centres.

Environmental Awareness Tips

  • Separate wet and dry waste.
  • Avoid single-use plastic products.
  • Carry reusable shopping bags.
  • Reuse materials whenever possible.
  • Save water and electricity.
  • Keep your surroundings clean.
